# Frame the problem, test a claim, revise from results

A synthetic teaching example. The main path is one analytical planning order, not a mandatory sequence stated by the source. Conditions and feedback may change the appropriate next tool.

Goal: A scoped problem, a checked claim and an informative small test.

## Frame the problem

Evidence: Material evidence

Input: Problem description and current constraints.

Action / meaning: Record the boundary, stakeholders, constraints and an observable success criterion.

Output: An explicit scope and criterion.

Check: Check that the criterion is observable and the boundary is explicit.

## Check the claim

Evidence: Material evidence

Input: A claim and its supporting observations.

Action / meaning: Separate observations from explanations and seek competing explanations and counterevidence.

Output: A traceable claim with its remaining uncertainty.

Check: Do not treat one observation as proof.

methods-demo.txt · Lines 6-7

## Run a small informative test

Evidence: Material evidence

Input: An important assumption and a prediction.

Action / meaning: Run a low-cost test, compare its result with the prediction and revise the assumption before scaling.

Output: New information for the next decision.

Check: Compare result and prediction before scaling.
